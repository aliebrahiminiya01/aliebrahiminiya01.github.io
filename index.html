01

Brain tumor segmentation in MRI

PyTorch · MONAI · 2D U-Net · 484 patients

A U-Net that labels every pixel of a brain MRI as edema, non-enhancing tumor or enhancing tumor, trained on the Medical Segmentation Decathlon brain tumor set. I split the data by patient rather than by slice — otherwise neighbouring slices of the same person leak into the test set and the scores look better than they are.

Table 1 Dice score on the validation set and on 74 unseen test patients.

Validation0.9160.8660.877
Test0.9030.8600.851

Failure analysis: the three worst test slices all come from one patient, where the tumor is barely visible in any of the four MRI sequences and the scan has a strong intensity bias.

02

Predicting meal no-shows at a university restaurant

scikit-learn · pandas · 52,333 reservations

About one in seven reserved meals is never picked up. A first model reached 85% accuracy — the same as always predicting “picked up” — and caught 51 of 1,563 no-shows.

Adding each student's past pickup rate (computed only from earlier days) and testing on the last weeks of the semester instead of a random split raised ROC AUC from 0.55 to 0.66. On holidays more than half of reserved meals are not collected.

03

Hotel booking analysis

pandas · Matplotlib · 119,390 bookings

Two years of bookings from a city hotel and a resort in Portugal. Cleaning: about 32,000 duplicate rows removed, an invalid €5,400 room rate dropped, and revenue computed as nightly rate × nights for non-cancelled stays.

Bookings made within a week are cancelled 8% of the time; bookings made months ahead, around 40%.

scikit-learn · Tkinter · logistic regression + random forest

Desktop app that predicts loan approval with two models. If they disagree, the application is sent for manual review. On the test set the models approve almost all good applicants and about 40% of the risky ones.
