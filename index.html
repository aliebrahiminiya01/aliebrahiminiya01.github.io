01

Brain tumor segmentation in MRI

PyTorch · MONAI · 2D U-Net · 484 patients

A U-Net that labels every pixel of a brain MRI as edema, non-enhancing tumor or enhancing tumor, trained on the Medical Segmentation Decathlon brain tumor set. I split the data by patient rather than by slice — otherwise neighboring slices of the same person leak into the test set and the scores look better than they are.

02

Glioma grade prediction with radiomics

PyRadiomics · scikit-learn · BraTS 2020 · 369 patients

Low- vs high-grade glioma from 637 radiomics features computed inside the tumor masks. In cross-validation every feature set reaches ROC AUC around 0.95 — but in BraTS 2020 the contributing institution almost determines the grade.

Texture features identify the institution's scanner with AUC 0.99 among high-grade patients alone. On the 39 patients whose source contains both grades, texture models drop to 0.88, while a LASSO model on tumor shape holds at 0.93.

03

Survival analysis of diffuse glioma

lifelines · scikit-survival · TCGA LGG + GBM · 1,040 patients

Overall survival from seven clinical and molecular features (age, grade, IDH/1p19q subtype, Karnofsky score and others), with a held-out test set and nested cross-validation.

A Cox model reached Uno's C-index 0.81 and integrated Brier score 0.118 on the test set (no-information baseline 0.220). Random survival forest and gradient boosting did not beat it. Median survival is 14 months for IDH-wildtype tumors and about 116 months for IDH-mutant, 1p/19q-codeleted ones.

04

Automatic vs. expert masks for radiomics

PyTorch · MONAI · PyRadiomics · lifelines · BraTS 2020 · 369 patients

Projects 01–03 joined into one pipeline: the U-Net draws the tumor mask, radiomics features are computed from it, and the features go into the grade and survival models. The U-Net was retrained in 3 folds so every patient's mask comes from a model that never saw them.

The U-Net segments the whole tumor well (median Dice 0.91) but marks a small enhancing region in 26 of the 27 low-grade tumors that have none. A grade model trained on U-Net masks still reaches AUC 0.92 (0.95 with expert masks); one trained on expert masks and applied to U-Net masks drops to 0.85. For survival, tumor shape adds little to age with either mask type.

05

Predicting meal no-shows at a university restaurant

scikit-learn · pandas · 52,333 reservations

About one in seven reserved meals is never picked up. A first model reached 85% accuracy — the same as always predicting “picked up” — and caught 51 of 1,563 no-shows.

Adding each student's past pickup rate (computed only from earlier days) and testing on the last weeks of the semester instead of a random split raised ROC AUC from 0.55 to 0.66. On holidays more than half of reserved meals are not collected.

06

Hotel booking analysis

pandas · Matplotlib · 119,390 bookings

Two years of bookings from a city hotel and a resort in Portugal. Cleaning: about 32,000 duplicate rows removed, an invalid €5,400 room rate dropped, and revenue computed as nightly rate × nights for non-cancelled stays.

Bookings made within a week are cancelled 8% of the time; bookings made months ahead, around 40%.
