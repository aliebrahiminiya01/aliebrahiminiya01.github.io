Featured project

Brain Tumor Segmentation in MRI with U-Net

Pixel-wise segmentation of glioma sub-regions — edema, non-enhancing tumor and enhancing tumor — in multi-modal brain MRI, using a 2D U-Net trained on 484 patients from the Medical Segmentation Decathlon (BraTS).

0.903Whole tumor
0.860Tumor core
0.851Enhancing tumor
  • Patient-level train / validation / test split to prevent data leakage
  • Dice + cross-entropy loss for strong class imbalance; trained on Apple Silicon (MPS)
  • Failure analysis of the worst test cases, with limitations and next steps
  • PyTorch

About
